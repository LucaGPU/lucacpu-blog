Console Modding

Consoles other than the Wii that I have modded are the Wii U, 3DS, PS3, PSVita, Switch and despite not being a console im counting it anyway, The Kindle. The Wii U still has yet to be figured out and so much of the software and hardware is an engima. The PS3 however has mostly been figured out, Though I've not done much on it aside from modding GTAV.

The PSVita and 3DS for me are much different stories. Being handheld systems, I was able to tinker with them constantly while on my way to school or while on holiday. The PSVita was the first system I hacked. I installed a PSP system menu to play PSP homebrew and use PSP software and of course I installed PSP and PSVita roms I found online.

The 3DS on the other hand I messed with for years. I installed so many 3DS roms (also known as .CIA files) and themed the system so much using a homebrew theming tool.

The kindle and the switch were the hardest devices I have had the displeasure of modding. The Kindle software is largely unknown except for that we know its linux based. And so not much software is made for it. It took me about 10 attempts to succesfully mod the thing and even afterwards its hit or miss on wheather the device will load my custom e-reading app called koreader.

The Switch. The Switch is such a console of all time. having to reinstall the system sofware onto an SD card in order to be able to use the thing online and have it modded. combine that with the instability of all the homebrew software out there and lack of documentation caused many hours of confusion and torment. But it was worth it in the end when I got a version of SteamOS running on it.
