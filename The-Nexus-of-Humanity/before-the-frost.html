If you want the more subtle storytelling inside the actual book do not read further!!!

There are two types of people in this world. Purebloods and Newbloods (often refered to as Inhumans). Newbloods were people with extraordinary abilities. People hated the Newbloods for being differnt and would discriminate against them and at times even kill them. When the frost came many were quick to blame the Newbloods But as the world got colder and food shortages heightened. Many looked toward the Newbloods for help, but by this point it was too late and on June 25th London would fall. London falling was the event that caused most other settlements to crumble too, and those who survived would eventually get destroyed by raiders. But soon, a city would form that would bring hope in the eyes of everyone in what remained of britain. L'Santialla!
